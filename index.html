Our Story

Crafted for Those Who Refuse Ordinary

VELURA was founded in 2014 with a singular vision: to create clothing that bridges the gap between timeless elegance and modern sensibility. What began as a small atelier in Milan has grown into a global brand worn by those who value substance over trends.

Every piece in our collection begins with fabric. Our team travels to the finest mills across Italy, Japan, and France — hand-selecting Italian Merino Wool, Japanese Selvedge Denim, Mongolian Cashmere, and Mulberry Silk. We believe that exceptional clothing starts with exceptional materials.

Our commitment extends beyond aesthetics. We partner with ethical manufacturers who share our values — fair labour, sustainable practices, and zero-waste production. From GOTS-certified organic cottons to FSC-certified TENCEL™ Lyocell, every choice we make is intentional.
